### 🟠 About
Founded in 2017 by **Ibrahim Elshahawy**, an accounts manager who turns ten years of daily accounting work into software.
Our flagship product is **Qaswara ERP** — a cloud ERP & POS for restaurants, trading companies, clinics and 20+ industries
(automatic journals, inventory & recipes, delivery-apps reconciliation, HR & payroll, ZATCA e-invoicing).
Qaswara ERP is commercial — [try the live demo](https://qaswaraelfohrar.com). Everything below is **free and open source**.
